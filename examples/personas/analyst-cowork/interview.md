# Interview: Sam, bid analyst (Claude Cowork)

Simulated for the example. Answers are in the person's words; Gestalt's survey filled the
rest.

**Survey, read back to Sam.** About 570 always-on tokens (see [survey/SURVEY.md](survey/SURVEY.md)) from three org-synced plugins
with 22 skills between them, and their admin plans to push about twenty plugins this year.
One skill description shouts "ALWAYS USE this skill for any document". The platform has its
own Stop hook. Plugin counters show org-rfp used 3 times and the other two never.

## 1. Who and what for

- "I write RFP responses and the pricing models behind them. Some weeks it's all RFPs, some
  weeks it's all spreadsheets."
- Goals:
  1. "Get a first-draft RFP response with citations out in a day, not a week."
  2. "Stop the brand plugin rewriting my financial models into marketing copy."
- A bad day: "Claude picks the wrong plugin. I ask for a cost table and it gives me brand
  guidelines."

## 2. Where you work

- Claude Cowork on the desktop only. The org admin configures plugins; Sam can switch them
  off per folder but cannot uninstall them.

## 3. Data and tools

- SharePoint and Outlook (Microsoft 365) for past proposals and client mail; Confluence and
  Jira for the proposal library. Source of truth for past answers: **Confluence**.
- Credentials are org SSO; no keys to manage.

## 4. Kinds of work

- RFP folders have an `RFP.md` brief. Pricing folders have a `MODEL.md` with the model's
  assumptions.

## 5. Cadence

- "Monday I triage the week's bids. Nothing else is regular."

## 6. Pains

- "The brand skill fires on everything." (the survey's trigger finding)
- "I have twenty plugins and use two."
