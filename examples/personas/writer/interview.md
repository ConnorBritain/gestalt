# Interview: Ade, newsletter writer

Simulated for the example. Answers are in Ade's words.

**Survey, read back.** The Claude desktop app with three writing plugins from a
marketplace (about 2,500 always-on tokens, two of them generators that both claim
"writing"), a Google Drive connector, nothing else.

## 1. Who and what for

- "A weekly newsletter on the economics of work, and a book proposal."
- Goals:
  1. "Every issue sounds like me, not like a model, and I spend my time on ideas, not
     sanding sentences."
  2. "Know whether my instincts about headlines are any good."
- A bad day: "Three tools rewrite the same paragraph three ways, and none of them is mine."

## 2. Where you work

- Claude desktop and Claude Code in a folder of Markdown drafts. No Codex, no Cursor.

## 3. Data and tools

- Drafts in a Git folder; research links in Google Drive; publishing through the
  newsletter platform's API. Source of truth: **the drafts folder**.

## 4. Kinds of work

- The newsletter folder, and a `BOOK.md` folder for the proposal.

## 5. Cadence

- "Thursday I draft, Friday I edit, Monday I look at open rates."

## 6. Pains

- "Two drafting tools fighting over my voice."
- "Research I can't trace back to a source."
