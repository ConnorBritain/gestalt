# The interview

Six short blocks. Pre-fill every option you can from the survey and the existing way;
ask only what neither can tell you. Stop a block as soon as you have enough.

## 1. Who and what for

- What is your work: roles, the kinds of things you produce? (code, documents, decks,
  analysis, writing, client work, management)
- **At most three goals** for the next quarter. A goal says what will exist or be true, not
  an activity. Push back once on a vague one ("be more productive" → "ship the beta to ten
  users").
- What does a good day with your harness look like? A bad one?

## 2. Where you work

- Harnesses: Claude Code (terminal, desktop, web), Claude Cowork, Codex (CLI or cloud),
  Cursor, others reading AGENTS.md.
- Local, cloud, or both. Several machines?
- Who else configures your harness? (an org pushing plugins, a team repo's settings)

## 3. Data and tools

- Which systems hold the truth: tracker (Linear, Jira, GitHub Issues, Todoist), docs
  (Confluence, Notion, Google Drive, SharePoint), mail and calendar, chat, code hosting,
  analytics.
- **Which one is the source of truth** when two disagree?
- Credentials go in environment variables. Ask for the variable *name* only, never a value.

## 4. Kinds of work (contexts)

- Which kinds of repo or folder do you switch between? Name each by something on disk
  that marks it (`package.json`, `BRAND.md`, `docs/roadmap/roadmap.yaml`, `.prose/`).
- For each: what must always happen there, and what must never load there?

## 5. Cadence

- What do you already do daily, weekly, monthly (planning, review, retro, reporting)?
- Which of those should the harness prompt, and which should it leave alone?

## 6. Pains

Read the survey's collisions back to them and ask which ones they have felt. Then:

- What fires too often, or nags?
- What never fires that should?
- What do you have installed but never use? (cross-check with usage counters)

Close by reading back the goals and the source of truth in their words, and confirm.
